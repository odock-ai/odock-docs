# Add models from the catalog

Use the catalog when the model is already listed. The list includes approved Odock Catalog models plus your synced or manually added entries, with duplicates removed. See [Automatic Odock Catalog Availability](/docs/models-and-mcp/model-catalog/automatic-availability).

<Steps>

<Step>
Open your organisation in Odock.
</Step>

<Step>
Go to **Models**.
</Step>

<Step>
Click **Batch Create**.

</Step>

<Step>
Select the provider key for these models.
</Step>

<Step>
Search or filter models by name, slug, type, or capability.

</Step>

<Step>
Select the models you want.
</Step>

<Step>
Click **Create Selected Models**.
</Step>

</Steps>

Created models appear on the provider detail page and in **Models & MCP Servers**.


![Add models from the models catalog](/gifs/setup-add-models.gif)

If the model is not listed, sync it into your organisation catalog first. See [Syncing Into Your Organisation](/docs/models-and-mcp/model-catalog/sync-to-your-organisation).
